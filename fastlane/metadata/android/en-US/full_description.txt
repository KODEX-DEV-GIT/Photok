<b>PrivaCam – Secure Photo Vault, Hide Photos &amp; Videos</b>

PrivaCam is a secure private photo vault that helps you hide photos and videos using strong AES-256 encryption. Protect your sensitive media, create an encrypted gallery, and keep your private memories safe on your own device.

All files are encrypted locally and only decrypted in memory while you use the app. No cloud upload is required.

PrivaCam is ad-free and built with a privacy-first philosophy. Your data stays on your device, always.

• Hide photos and videos securely
• Private encrypted gallery with AES-256 protection
• Offline vault — your files stay on your device
• Transparent security architecture
• Ad-free user experience
• Built for privacy-conscious users

<b>Vault Features</b>
• Import photos &amp; videos from your gallery
• Organize private media into albums
• Export files anytime
• Create and restore encrypted backups
• Unlock your vault with fingerprint authentication
• Share media directly to the vault
• Option to hide the app icon
• Automatically delete original files after import

<b>Privacy-Focused Design</b>
PrivaCam is designed for people who want real control over their private photos and videos.
